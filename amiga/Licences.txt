The data
--------
Everything here comes from OpenLieroX's own repository (share/gamedir),
as OpenLieroX distributes it with the game, under the same terms:

  data/          the game's graphics, sounds, fonts and menus
  Classic/       the "Classic" mod that Liero Xtreme came with (enhanced
                 graphics by Strider, see Classic/gfx/readme.txt)
  levels/        the levels that come with OpenLieroX
  skins/         a few of the worm skins that come with OpenLieroX
  cfg/           the default profiles, weapon restrictions and taunts
  default.css, default.png
                 the default style sheet and font

  data/flags/    flag icons from famfamfam.com, public domain
                 (data/flags/readme.txt)

Left out of the drawer (they stay in the repository): the other mods,
levels and skins that are drawn after other games, films and characters
(Zelda, Mario, Naruto, Star Wars, Simpsons and the like), the dedicated
server's scripts, the server lists, the GeoIP database and the Commander
Keen tile tables. Mods and levels from the repository, or from the
OpenLieroX community, can be copied into the drawer and play as they are.
